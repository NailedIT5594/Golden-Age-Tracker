# The Golden Age — Elden Ring Companion App

*Welcome to **The Golden Age Companion App**!* This is a lightweight, fully interactive progress tracker and field guide built specifically for *The Golden Age* Elden Ring mod. 

Whether you are running it live via **GitHub Pages** or simply double-clicking it on your desktop, everything runs *100% client-side* right in your web browser—no external servers, backend databases, or complex setup required.

---

## Quick Start

- **Live Deployment:** Head over to your *GitHub Pages* URL in any modern web browser.
- **Local Desktop Use:** Simply open `index.html` (or `The_Golden_Age_Interactive_Checklist.html`) directly from your project folder.

---

## How to Use It

- **Track Your Journey:** Click through the sidebar navigation to check off *bosses*, *rewards*, *prerequisites*, and *hidden items* as you explore the mod.
- **Enable Audio:** *Click anywhere on the page* when you first launch the app so your browser permits audio cues and achievement sounds to play.
- **Saving & Backups:** All your progress saves *automatically* to your browser's local storage. To transfer your data to another device or browser, visit the **Settings & Data** menu to export or import a backup JSON file.

---

## Features & Highlights

### Achievement System & Dynamic Audio
- **30+ Tiered Achievements:** Spanning *Common*, *Uncommon*, *Rare*, *Epic*, *Legendary*, and *hidden secret* milestones.
- **Dynamic Rarity Audio:** Features distinct sound effects mapped directly to each achievement rarity tier.
- **100% Completion Celebration:** Includes a unique audio cue (`100%.mp3`) that triggers *exclusively* when every single achievement is unlocked.
- **Full Management:** Search, filter, sort, pin favorites, and review saved unlock notifications at any time.

### Boss Intel & Tracker Catalog
- **Expanded Database:** Complete coverage for **50 mod bosses**, *25 prerequisite chains*, *77 unique reward IDs* across *9 categories*, and *25 weapon records*.
- **Interactive Boss Cards:** Click any boss card to reveal exact locations, prerequisites, drops, verification notes, and direct journal shortcuts.
- **Clear Status Tracking:** Bosses are automatically categorized as **Defeated**, **Available**, **Locked**, or **Unknown**.

### Hidden Talismans Field Guide
- **Dedicated Tracker:** Full tracking page for *8 canonical mod talismans* complete with custom SVG emblem artwork.
- **Smart Dependency Checks:** Tracks independent collection states while verifying prerequisite dependencies for end-game items like *Eccentric Corpse*.

### Journey Journal & Field Notes
- **Adventure Logging:** Record field notes, boss encounters, and reminders with custom *tags*, *dates*, *importance ratings*, and *pinned states*.
- **One-Click Shortcuts:** Prefill journal entries instantly straight from boss cards, area descriptions, or achievements.

### Deep Visual Customization
- **10 Dark-Fantasy Themes:** Personalize your experience with custom accent colors, typography, background atmosphere, borders, card styles, and *reduced-motion preferences*.

---

## Data & Backups

- **Automatic Local Storage:** All progress, journal logs, pinned items, and user preferences save directly in your browser's `localStorage`.
- **Seamless Portability:** Use the **Settings & Data** menu anytime to download a `.json` backup file and safely transfer your progress across browsers or devices.
